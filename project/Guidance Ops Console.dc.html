How a lead reaches a person

Every record in this console follows these four steps.

  1. 1

    The assistant captures the lead. It answers financing questions on the website and asks for name, email, phone, location and timeline.

  2. 2

    Routing reads the US state and picks one of five regional GHS Concierge owners. Agents applying to the referral network go to the agent desk instead, never to a region.

  3. 3

    A handoff email goes to that owner, copying the concierge team. If it is still queued, nobody has been told yet.

  4. 4

    The owner replies within the target: one hour for homebuyers, three for agents. Past that, the lead shows as late.

Who handles what

Two separate teams. A lead belongs to one track, never both.
